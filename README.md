# RapidRAW Presets by niksmac

A curated collection of [RapidRAW](https://www.getrapidraw.com) presets designed for nature, wildlife, landscape, and portrait photography. Tuned for mirrorless cameras (Sony A6400) and mobile RAW workflows.

## Presets

| File | Best for | Style |
|------|----------|-------|
| `Natural` | All-purpose | Clean, true-to-life with subtle contrast and a gentle s-curve |
| `Natural-Portrait` | People & portraits | Warm skin tones, soft highlights, gentle clarity |
| `Cinematic` | Storytelling frames | Teal shadows, warm orange highlights, film-like s-curve |
| `Bright-Airy` | Lifestyle, pastels | High-key, lifted blacks, soft desaturated tones |
| `Warm-Golden-Hour` | Sunset, golden light | Warm temperature, boosted oranges/yellows, slight grain |
| `Landscape-Vibrant` | Scenery, outdoors | Rich greens and blues, high vibrance, dehaze |
| `Wildlife-Detail` | Animals, birds | High sharpness, clarity, and structure for feather/fur detail |
| `Moody-Nature` | Forest, overcast | Deep shadows, desaturated greens, atmospheric vignette |

## Camera & Lens Notes

Built around the **Sony A6400** (APS-C) with:

- Sony 70-350mm F4.5-6.3 G OSS (wildlife)
- Sony 50mm F1.8 (portraits)
- Sony E PZ 18-105mm F4 G OSS (general)

Also compatible with iPhone 14 Pro RAW and any mirrorless/DSLR camera.

## Installation

1. Copy the `presets/` folder and `manifest.json` into your RapidRAW presets directory.
2. Open RapidRAW and apply a preset from the presets panel.
3. Fine-tune exposure to match your specific shot — all presets are designed as starting points.

### Manual import

Open any `.rrpreset` file directly from Files or the share sheet into RapidRAW.

## About RapidRAW

[RapidRAW](https://www.getrapidraw.com) is a non-destructive, GPU-accelerated RAW photo editor by Timon Käch. Supports Apple ProRAW, mirrorless camera RAW files, AI masking, and a powerful preset system.
